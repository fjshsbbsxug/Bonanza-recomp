Bonanza Bros Port Builder

Turn your own copy of the Mega Drive game into a native Windows, Linux or Android port. The game's code is made from your ROM in this page; the ROM stays on your device and nothing is uploaded.

Bonanza Maker: build your own stages Mario Maker style, test-play them, and save them as .bbro mods.

This page is the builder's source. Use one of the builder pages instead (BonanzaBuilder-Windows.html, BonanzaBuilder-Linux.html, BonanzaBuilder-Android.html, from the project's Releases page): each has its platform's engine inside it and makes the game's code from your ROM.

Player characters

Give Robo (player 1) and Mobo (player 2) their own shirt, pants and skin tone. Only the two player characters change; guards and the rest of the game keep their colors.

Your own colors

Replace any color of the whole game with one you choose, for example the walls, floors or the HUD. Start the live preview, then press Pick a color in the game: the game's Recolor menu shows the colors on screen and flashes the one you select. Your picks appear in this list.

Recolor changes a color everywhere it appears; use Player characters to change only Robo and Mobo.

Built from the bonanza-recomp project. Nothing of the game is in this page or in the engine it carries: the game's 68000 code is translated to C from your ROM right here (the project's recompiler, run by Pyodide, MPL-2.0) and compiled by TinyCC (LGPL-2.1, compiled to WebAssembly), and its graphics and sounds come from the ROM when the game runs.
